# Adding demonstration media

1. Preserve original recordings and record their hashes privately.
2. Establish rights, policy identity where available, and whether footage is
   simulation or hardware. Keep unknowns explicit.
3. Select continuous excerpts at original timeline speed. Retain visible tethers,
   handlers and relevant obstacle geometry; never interpolate robot motion.
4. Record source hashes, excerpt times, derivative hashes, dimensions and frame
   timing. Keep private workstation paths out of public provenance.
5. Recheck captions, links, payload and disclosure boundaries before publication.
   Existing permissions do not automatically cover future additions.

The original preparation manifest remains immutable. Later optimized derivatives
are recorded separately in [media-optimization-v1.json](media-optimization-v1.json).
