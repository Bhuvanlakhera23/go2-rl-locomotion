# Experimental identity and terminology

**Foundation-02B** is the internal experimental identifier used in the historical
source, artifact paths and checkpoint provenance. It is not a generic controller
name or an operation performed by the learned actor.

Public demonstrations use **blind high-obstacle locomotion policy**. Its training
intervention is described as **a policy trained using a fixed task mixture**:
task selection allocates experience during training. During locomotion, the actor
maps its observations and motion history to actions; it does not sample training
tasks.

Quantitative research comparisons retain the established **Fixed-Mixture Policy**
and **Adaptive-Frontier Policy** identities. See the report's
[policy identity mapping](https://github.com/Bhuvanlakhera23/go2-obstacle-learning-rl/blob/main/docs/POLICY_IDENTITY_MAPPING.md)
and the original [media provenance](media-provenance.json). Existing filenames,
checkpoint/source hashes, historical manifests and the frozen report/release are
retained verbatim for traceability. They are not renamed or rewritten to match
the current presentation terminology.
